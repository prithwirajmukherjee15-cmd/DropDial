# Changelog

What changed in each version of DropDial, written for the people using it.

## 1.1.0

**Many more kinds of file**
- Spreadsheets: Excel, CSV, TSV and OpenDocument to Excel, CSV, JSON, HTML, PDF, Word and Markdown.
- Data: JSON, YAML, TOML, XML, property lists and vCard contacts, to each other, CSV and Excel.
- Documents: Markdown, HTML, saved web pages and e-mails (.eml) to PDF, Word, HTML and text; save an e-mail's attachments.
- Subtitles: SRT, WebVTT, ASS, SBV, MicroDVD, SAMI, LRC and TTML to each other; clean up captions and fix PAL/NTSC timing.
- Archives: extract or repack ZIP, 7Z and TAR files; a ZIP slice for several files or a whole folder.
- Fonts: TTF, OTF, WOFF and WOFF2 to each other, a specimen sheet, and a Latin subset for the web.
- 3D models: STL, OBJ, PLY, GLB, OFF and 3MF to each other, plus a rendered preview.
- Map data: GPX, KML, KMZ and GeoJSON to each other and CSV; trip stats, reverse and simplify a route.
- Pro images: camera RAW (Canon, Nikon, Sony, Fujifilm, DNG…), Photoshop PSD, TGA, QOI, JPEG 2000 and more.
- Images can also become SVG (traced vectors), ICNS (Mac icons), TGA and QOI.

**New in the app**
- Home: click the tray / menu-bar icon for a drop zone (no Shift needed) and your recent results with previews.
- Drag a picture, a file link or a download straight out of your browser onto the dial.
- The dial has more sides: past 8 formats the rest move to "More formats". Flip with Shift, Tab or the mouse wheel.
- Pick an accent colour in Settings: Champagne, Rose, Ocean, Emerald, Violet or Silver.
- DropDial tells you when a new version is out (Settings → Check for updates turns this off).
- Opening DropDial again shows Home instead of a notice.

**Fixes**
- The dial shows its slices the moment it opens, even if you hold the mouse still.
- A folder of results now shows its real size in the result card.

## 1.0.0

First release as DropDial: Shift-drag any file, drop it on the dial to convert, compress or edit it.
Images, PDF, Word, text, SVG, e-books, video and audio; recipes, watch folders, history and a
`dropdial` command. Windows, macOS and Linux.
