# Code of Conduct

DropDial follows the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1, adapted below for a small project.

## Our pledge

We want taking part in DropDial to be a good experience for everyone, whatever their age, body size, visible or
invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that make this an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Our standards

Things that help:

- Being kind and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Respecting different opinions, viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving helpful feedback, and accepting it gracefully.
- Owning up to mistakes, apologising to the people affected, and learning from them.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and the people who use it.

Things that are not acceptable:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insults, derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Harassment, in public or in private.
- Publishing someone else's private information, such as a home or email address, without their clear permission.
- Any other behaviour that would reasonably be seen as inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

The project owner, Prithwiraj Mukherjee, is responsible for explaining and enforcing these standards. The owner
will take fair action in response to any behaviour they find inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful.

The owner may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues and other contributions that don't
follow this Code of Conduct, and will explain the reason when it's appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, reviews, commit messages and any other
place where DropDial is discussed. It also applies when someone officially represents the project in public.

## Reporting

If you experience or see behaviour that breaks this Code of Conduct, please tell the owner privately through
GitHub: [github.com/prithwirajmukherjee15-cmd](https://github.com/prithwirajmukherjee15-cmd). If the problem is
in an issue or pull request, you can also use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment.

Every report will be reviewed and looked into promptly and fairly. The privacy and safety of the person who reports
an incident will be respected.

## Enforcement guidelines

The owner will follow these guidelines when deciding what to do:

### 1. Correction

**Impact:** inappropriate language or other behaviour that is unprofessional or unwelcome.

**Consequence:** a private, written warning that explains what happened and why it was inappropriate. A public
apology may be requested.

### 2. Warning

**Impact:** a single serious incident, or a series of smaller ones.

**Consequence:** a warning with consequences for continued behaviour. For a set period, no interaction with the
people involved, including unrequested interaction with the people enforcing this Code of Conduct. Breaking these
terms may lead to a temporary or permanent ban.

### 3. Temporary ban

**Impact:** a serious breach of the standards, including ongoing inappropriate behaviour.

**Consequence:** a temporary ban from any interaction or public communication with the project for a set period.
No public or private interaction with the people involved is allowed during this time. Breaking these terms may
lead to a permanent ban.

### 4. Permanent ban

**Impact:** a pattern of breaking the standards, including ongoing inappropriate behaviour, harassment of a
person, or aggression toward or belittling of groups of people.

**Consequence:** a permanent ban from any public interaction with the project.
